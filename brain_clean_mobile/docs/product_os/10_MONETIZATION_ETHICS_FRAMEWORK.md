# 10 — Monetization Ethics Framework

**Purpose:** Revenue without exploiting distress. Free core must remain genuinely useful.

---

## Free-core promise

Free users can:

- Run Daily Program  
- Close the day (Day End)  
- Access essential calm/mood paths needed for today’s journey  
- Use crisis/SOS paths without paywall  

**PRODUCT DECISION:** Free is not a broken demo of recovery.

---

## Pro value proposition (ethical)

Sell:

- Peace (ad removal)  
- Deeper personalization / coaching continuity  
- Backup/sync when enabled  
- Richer insights/reports  
- Premium polish (themes, etc.)

Do **not** sell:

- Escape from shame you created  
- Access to crisis help  
- “Cure” packages  
- Cognitive enhancement fantasy

---

## Ads rules

**FACT (handoff):** Banner-only; UMP consent; Pro removes ads; exclusions on sensitive routes.  

**PRODUCT DECISION:** Never show ads on:

- Daily Program  
- Day End  
- Safa conversations  
- Emotion / worry flows  
- Focus/calm intervention screens  
- SOS / relapse / recovery-critical paths  
- Paywall itself  

Allowed (with caution): Home, Exercises, Journey, More — monitor trust.

**Risk:** Recovery brand + ads can reduce perceived integrity even when compliant.

---

## Paywall timing

Preferred: after value (e.g., multi-day DP completions), from More/Pro, honest trial end.  
Rejected: mid-step emotional vulnerability, forced to finish mood, after relapse logging.

---

## Trial rules

- Clear start/end  
- No dark pattern “gotcha”  
- Restore purchases always available  

---

- Local purchasing power

Evidence for optimal price: **Evidence verification required** (run experiments).

---

## Safa monetization boundaries

- Contextual help during DP should not hard-block free completion paths  
- Deeper ongoing chat may be Pro  
- Never Pro-gate crisis escalation content  

---

## What must remain free (minimum)

Daily Program core, Day End, restricted-route safety, basic mood for DP, essential breathing/calm needed to continue today.

---

## Ethical upsell examples

- “Remove banners and keep your space calm.”  
- “Unlock deeper weekly insights.”  
- «أزل الإعلانات وخلك بفضاء أهدأ.»

## Rejected dark patterns

- Fake countdown urgency  
- Guilt paywalls  
- Disguised ads as features  
- Forced account for basic DP  
- “Diagnose then pay to fix”

---

## Metrics

Revenue: trials start, conversion, refund rate, LTV.  
Trust: ad complaints, uninstall after paywall, restricted-route ad impressions (target 0).
