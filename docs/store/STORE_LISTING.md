## Full description — EN
Do you open your phone without meaning to, then realize an hour has gone?
Brain Clean helps you build a calmer relationship with your phone, one small step a day.

The Clarity Program — 30 days, 4 stages
• Week 1: Awareness — notice when and why you reach for your phone.
• Week 2: Environment — change your surroundings so scrolling takes more effort.
• Week 3: Replace — prepare real alternatives for urge moments.
• Week 4: Lock in — create your own rules to keep going after the program.

Every day: a short lesson + a practical exercise + a simple challenge. Just 5 minutes.

SOS button — always free
Strong urge to scroll? Bored? Stressed? Can't sleep? One minute of breathing and guidance helps you ride out the moment.

Clarity Check
8 quick questions on focus, control, sleep and calm. Retake it on days 7, 14, 21 and 30 to see your progress clearly. (Not a medical assessment.)

Tools that help
• Focus timer with calm sounds.
• Breathing exercises.
• A sorted exercise library.
• Your real phone screen time (optional, with your permission, stays on your device).
• 20-20-20 eye-rest reminder.

Your progress, at a glance
Clarity chart, screen time, day calendar, days practiced, and focus minutes.

Privacy first
Your data stays on your device, encrypted. No account, no ads.

The first 7 days and the SOS button are free. Unlock the full program with a monthly, yearly (with a free trial) or lifetime plan. Cancel anytime in Google Play.
