## English — Closed Testing / future store notes

### What’s new in Brain Clean 2.0

Brain Clean 2.0 introduces a calmer recovery journey focused on understanding your current state, building a practical plan, and observing progress over time.

- Complete Brain Check and see an explainable Recovery Score estimate and Brain Profile.
- Receive a personal Recovery Plan with clear daily actions.
- Use Today and short daily sessions with optional lighter paths.
- Review Progress and weekly reflections with honest, limited-evidence language.
- Access Premium features after proof moments (subscription required where gated).
- Open Safa for brief, contextual support when you feel stuck.
- Full Arabic and English support with RTL/LTR layouts.

Brain Clean is not a medical diagnosis or treatment. Progress can vary.
